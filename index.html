01

Product Overview & Identity

What it is

4-Nitrophthalic acid (4-NPA) is a benzene ring carrying two adjacent carboxylic acid groups and a nitro group para to one of them. It is the positional isomer of 3-nitrophthalic acid and shares its molecular formula. Commercial value comes from reducing the nitro group or building on the anhydride, which gives routes to fluorescein labels, 4-aminophthalic acid, isoluminol-type reagents and polyimide monomers.[42][43][44] It is a low-volume fine chemical, not a bulk commodity.

Customer-side hazard: the dry fusion of 4-NPA with resorcinol can turn into an uncontrollable, violent exotherm above about 50-100 g, per a process patent. Technical support on fluorescein intermediates should steer buyers to the methanesulfonic acid route.[42]

India strategic note

India can make this. Feedstocks are domestic and the Gujarat nitration belt already runs the sister isomer. The commercial problem is not chemistry but outlet: isomer stream economics and small demand. A toll partner with 3-NPA capacity is the low-capital path.

Industry Applications, End-Products & Market Sizes

All market sizes below are downstream end markets, not 4-NPA demand. The product is a small input into each. Do not add them together or treat them as addressable revenue.

Diagnostics and fluorescent labels (strongest documented pull)

4-NPA condenses with resorcinol to give 5-/6-nitrofluorescein, the key intermediate for 5-aminofluorescein, FITC and fluorescein lisicol. One process patent runs this on a 600 g batch.[42] Separately, reduction gives 4-aminophthalic acid, and isoluminol is 4-aminophthalic acid hydrazide, the chemiluminescent label family used in immunoassays.[43][59]

Pharmaceutical impurity standards and intermediates

Listings confirm use as an impurity reference for apremilast, pomalidomide and dronedarone.[46][48] Both apremilast and pomalidomide are built on the 3-substituted series, so 4-NPA matters here as the wrong isomer to control. That means a steady but gram-scale need. SynZeal positions it for method validation, QC and ANDA filing.[38]

Specialty polymers and electronics

4-NPA dehydrates to 4-nitrophthalic anhydride with acetic anhydride (93% after recrystallisation, US 3,979,416 example).[64] US 4,808,731 cites prior art preparing oxydiphthalic anhydride (ODPA), a polyimide dianhydride, by heating that anhydride with sodium or potassium nitrite in a dipolar aprotic solvent.[44] Current ODPA plants may use other feedstocks, so this link is not assumed to be commercial volume.

Dyes, pigments, plasticizers and agrochemicals

The Sudarshan filing names pigments, dyes, PVC plasticizers, polyesters, polyamides, peptides and agricultural actives as uses, citing older patents.[40] Vikas lists agrochemical use.[21] Nothing reviewed names a current commercial agrochemical that consumes 4-NPA, so this is an unconfirmed outlet.

Research and materials

Used to build coordination polymers, for example a homochiral Mn framework from 4-nitrophthalate.[50] Screenshot flows are all this type of demand: laboratory packs.[10]

No evidence of use in food, flavour, fragrance, cosmetics or construction chemicals was found. These clusters are excluded.
